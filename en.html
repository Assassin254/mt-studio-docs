⚡ Chapter 3: 1-Click PBR Map Generator & Fabric Micro-Sheen

3.1. Common Issues with Web Textures

Most downloaded textures online are flat 8-bit RGB color images lacking Normal, Roughness, or AO maps. Rendering them results in artificial flatness and fake reflections.

3.2. 1-Click Complete PBR Map Generator

MT_PBR Studio Pro analyzes your single Base Color image algorithmically and automatically generates all 4 crucial architectural maps:

  • Normal Map (Sobel Algorithm): Extracts directional micro-surface angles for crisp 3D lighting.
  • Micro-Relief Bump: Adds fine tactile depth without geometry subdivision.
  • Calibrated Roughness Map: Distributes gloss and matte zones naturally across texture crevices.
  • Cavity Ambient Occlusion (AO): Bakes deep shadow crevices in grooved and textured surfaces.

3.3. Fabric & Leather Micro-Sheen (Zero Plastic Sheen)

Synthetic plastic reflections on sofas and cushions are completely eliminated through physical Micro-Sheen optical grazing calculation:

💡 What is Micro-Sheen?
Micro-Sheen simulates millions of microscopic fuzz fibers catching grazing light on fabric edges, providing soft velvety sheen at glancing angles while keeping facing surfaces natural and matte.

🌪️ Chapter 4: Surface Imperfections, Scratches & Alpha Controls

4.1. 3 Visual Alpha Calibration Sliders

Fine-tune surface dirt, water drip marks, and finger scratches with absolute precision:

Slider Property Function Description Recommended Practical Values
Coverage Density (Density) Filters faint dust haze; isolates distinct scratch marks. 0.00 (rich haze) to 0.35 (clean surfaces).
B/W Contrast (Contrast) Sharpens edge separation between dirt and clean areas. 0.00 (smooth blend) to 0.50 (crisp edge grime).
Alpha Brightness (Brightness) Scales the overall intensity and visibility of the imperfection layer. -0.20 (subtle gloss) to +0.30 (heavy weathering).

4.2. Smart Slope Filter: Keeping Cabinet Tops Clean

Toggle Clean Upward Faces to automatically exclude vertical dust from horizontal countertops and table tops, mimicking real-world gravity-driven dirt accumulation.

4.3. Interactive Mouse Transform Gizmo (Modal G / R)

Click 🎯 Transform Gizmo in the 3D Viewport:

  • Press G: Move the scratch/dirt map across the surface with mouse.
  • Press R: Rotate the scratch orientation seamlessly.
  • Press S: Scale the imperfection frequency up or down.
  • Click Left Mouse: Confirm new position • Esc: Cancel.
